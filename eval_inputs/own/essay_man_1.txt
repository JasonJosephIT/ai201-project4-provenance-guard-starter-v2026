Be a man. words that haunt the minds of many men today, words that confine us, words that limit and try to define us. they try to dictate our actions, emotions, and almost every aspect of our lifestyle. sometimes forcing us to live a life that we don't want, but one which is pushed on us by society standards and expectations. these assumptions placed upon my life and the lives of other men could scar, derail, and be a scourge on one's lives simply because society values us by how well we could be this three-letter word. being a man is a death sentence.

Should I be a man? should I throw away my emotions, should I ignore or bottle my struggles in life, should I refuse to show any sign of weakness except angry and violence, should I have to prove my manhood when it's challenged and when it's not?
