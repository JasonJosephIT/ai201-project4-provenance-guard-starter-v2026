There’s nothing wrong with telling someone to “be a man”, but being a man doesn’t come from having a Y chromosome but traits that learn from the environment around them, and the people that inhabit it. I have no problem with that phrase but it’s what comes after that disturbs me. Some tell their child to be a man but don’t show them how, they don’t show the compassion, vulnerability, kindness, humility, dedication or courage that goes into being a man, a good man in this day of age. Being a man isn’t something we just tell them to do but instruct them to do.
