Coffee culture has changed a lot over the past twenty years. What used to be a quick cup at the diner is now, for many people, a whole ritual: single-origin beans, pour-over kettles, oat milk. I'm not sure it's all better. Specialty shops have certainly raised the bar for quality, and baristas today know far more about extraction than they used to. But prices have climbed too, and a lot of neighborhoods lost the cheap corner spot where you could sit for an hour without anyone caring. Still, it's hard to argue with a really good cup. In the end, coffee culture reflects broader shifts in how we think about food, community and small luxuries.
