Regular exercise is one of the best things you can do for your health. It strengthens the heart, helps control weight, and can lift your mood on a bad day. You don't need a gym membership either. My neighbor walks the same loop around the park every morning and swears it's kept her knees working. Experts generally recommend about 150 minutes of moderate activity a week, which works out to roughly twenty minutes a day. That sounds like a lot until you count the stairs, the walk to the bus, carrying groceries. The key is consistency: pick something you actually enjoy, start small, and build from there.
